# Royal Veterinary College (RVC) – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

The Royal Veterinary College (RVC), established in 1791, is a specialist veterinary and biological sciences institution and a constituent member of the University of London. It provides undergraduate and postgraduate education, veterinary clinical services, scientific research and professional development.

The redesigned homepage focuses on serving three major purposes: attracting prospective students, connecting animal owners with clinical services and showcasing the institution's research achievements. The design aims to create clearer pathways for students, veterinary professionals, researchers and pet owners.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Audience-Specific Navigation
- Introduced prominent entry points for prospective students, current students, animal owners, veterinary professionals and researchers.
- Improved the visibility of audience-specific information and calls to action.
- Organised homepage navigation around the different needs of RVC's visitors.

**Problem addressed:** The homepage serves several distinct audiences, making it difficult for visitors to identify the most relevant information quickly.

### 2. Stronger Visibility for Clinical Services
- Created a dedicated clinical services section.
- Improved access to information about the Queen Mother Hospital for Animals, Beaumont Sainsbury Animal Hospital and RVC Equine.
- Provided clearer pathways to appointments, emergency care, hospital information and specialist veterinary services.

**Problem addressed:** RVC's public-facing veterinary facilities and clinical services lacked sufficient prominence within the overall homepage experience.

### 3. Greater Emphasis on Research and Academic Excellence
- Increased the visibility of research achievements and institutional expertise.
- Connected research capabilities, facilities and real-world impact with educational opportunities.
- Highlighted the institution's veterinary science reputation, including its reported 88% internationally excellent or world-leading research result in REF 2021.

**Problem addressed:** The connection between RVC's research reputation and its educational offering was not sufficiently prominent, limiting an important differentiator for prospective students.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Established a professional academic and scientific visual direction reflecting RVC's heritage and international reputation.
- **Homepage Layout:** Improved content hierarchy around education, clinical services and research.
- **Audience Navigation:** Created clearer pathways to help different visitor groups find relevant information.
- **Clinical Service Presentation:** Gave veterinary hospitals and clinical facilities a more visible position within the homepage structure.
- **User Experience:** Improved access to important information through more focused navigation and calls to action.
- **Content Consistency:** Connected academic programmes, clinical expertise and research achievements into a more cohesive institutional presentation.
- **Overall Appearance:** Developed a more organised, informative and audience-focused homepage that communicates RVC's role as an educational institution, clinical provider and research leader.

---

**Project Focus:** Clearer audience journeys, stronger clinical service visibility and better integration of research achievements with the student experience.
